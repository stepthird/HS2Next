Timeline compositions for the Timeline tests, written as the input of the `timeline.build` command (`duration`, `loop`, `tracks`; `all_types.json` also `events`). Node keys are placeholders that a test replaces with the ids of the nodes it creates: `$a` the female visual target of `Tools/targets.json`, `$b` the male visual target, `$p` a vanilla item (`item.add {no: 399}`), `$l` a light. `camera_dof.json` (2 tracks, 10 keys) drives the camera and the depth of field; `walk.json` (5 tracks, 28 keys) one character's position, rotation, animation and animation speed with the camera; `bones9.json` (10 tracks, 82 keys) nine FK bone rotations of two characters (FK on, groups Body and Neck) with the camera; `all_types.json` (15 tracks, 49 keys) one track of every track type, each focus form of the depth of field and every ease, and six timed commands at the start, the middle and the end. The animation rows are the non-H rows of the `animation` cases of `Tools/targets.json`. These files are dedicated to the public domain (CC0-1.0).
